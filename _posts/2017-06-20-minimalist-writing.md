<p>We are now in an age of information, too much of it.</p><p>We suffer not from lack of knowledge, but from overwhelming amount of data everyday.</p><p>Still, we write big articles, use fancy words, bring useless metaphors, reiterate same points in never-ending comments. A waste of time to both readers and writers.</p><p>This is a challenge to all of us, to:</p><ul><li>Use smaller words that carry more weight</li><li>Be honest with facts, while be kind in the message</li><li>Read before publish, trim out useless padding</li></ul><p>In the end, length is not proof of wisdom, but an inability to reduce information to its simplest form

</p>
